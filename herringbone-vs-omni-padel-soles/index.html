Herringbone vs omni padel soles: which should you choose?

The sole is the most important part of a padel shoe. The right pattern depends less on your level and more on the courts you play on.

Quick answer: Choose herringbone for sandy artificial turf, omni for indoor courts and courts with less sand, and hybrid if you play at several clubs with different courts.

Herringbone soles

A herringbone sole has a zig-zag pattern of grooves, like a clay-court tennis shoe. The grooves bite through loose sand into the turf, giving strong grip and lateral support while still letting you slide a little.

  • Best on: sandy artificial turf.
  • Watch out for: it can feel too grippy on harder courts with little sand.

Omni soles

An omni sole is covered in small, round studs. It moves freely in every direction and doesn't over-grip on firm surfaces.

  • Best on: indoor courts and courts with less sand.
  • Watch out for: less grip in loose sand, and the studs wear down faster.

Hybrid soles

Hybrid soles combine both patterns, usually herringbone under the forefoot for grip and omni studs elsewhere for freedom of movement. They're a sensible default if you play in lots of places.

  • Best on: mixed court types.
  • Watch out for: slightly less grip than a full herringbone on very sandy courts.

How to tell what your courts need

Look at the court surface before you play. If you can see a layer of sand on top of the turf, or your feet leave marks, go herringbone. If the turf looks firm and the sand has settled in, an omni sole will feel freer. Newly laid courts are often sandier than older ones.

Frequently asked questions

Which sole is best for outdoor padel in the UK?

It depends on the court rather than the weather. Many outdoor courts are sandy, which suits herringbone or hybrid soles.

Which padel sole lasts longest?

Herringbone soles generally last longer than omni soles, whose small studs wear down faster.

Can I use a herringbone sole indoors?

Yes, but on indoor courts with little sand it can feel grippy. Omni or hybrid soles usually feel better there.
