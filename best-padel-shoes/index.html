The best padel shoes in 2026, and how to choose

Running shoes are the most common beginner mistake. Padel is all sprints, stops and sideways moves on sandy artificial turf, and you need a shoe built for exactly that.

What to look for in a padel shoe

The right soleA herringbone, omni or hybrid tread grips sand-filled turf without locking your foot in place, which is how ankles and knees get hurt.
Lateral supportA firm upper and heel stop your foot rolling on quick side-steps.
Reinforced toeLow volleys drag your toe across the turf. A reinforced toe stops it wearing through.
CushioningPadel is played on a relatively hard surface. Good cushioning protects your heels and knees over a long session.

Which sole suits your court?

Padel shoes come with three main sole patterns. The right one depends on how much sand is on the courts you play on.

SolePatternBest on
HerringboneZig-zag groovesSandy artificial turf. Strong grip and lateral support, still lets you slide a little.
OmniSmall circular studsIndoor courts and courts with less sand. More freedom to move, but wears faster.
HybridHerringbone and omni combinedPlayers who switch between different clubs and court types.

Getting the fit right

  • Try shoes on later in the day, when your feet are slightly larger, and wear your playing socks.
  • Leave roughly a thumb's width at the toe. Padel involves lots of sudden stops, and a cramped toe box leads to black toenails.
  • Your heel shouldn't lift when you walk. Heel slip turns into blisters on a sandy court.
  • If you have wide feet, look for models sold in a wide fit rather than sizing up.

When to replace your padel shoes

Regular players usually wear through a pair in 6 to 12 months. Check the sole: when the herringbone grooves or omni studs have worn smooth under the ball of your foot, the shoe has lost its grip, and slipping is how injuries happen. Rotating two pairs makes both last longer.

Frequently asked questions

Do I really need padel shoes?

If you play regularly, yes. Padel shoes have soles designed to grip sandy artificial turf and uppers built for sideways movement. Running shoes have neither. Read can you play padel in running shoes?

Can I wear tennis shoes for padel?

Clay-court tennis shoes with a herringbone sole work reasonably well on padel courts. Hard-court tennis shoes are better than running shoes but usually grip less well on sand.

Which padel shoe sole is best?

Herringbone for sandy courts, omni for indoor or less sandy courts, and hybrid if you play at different clubs.

How long do padel shoes last?

Around 6 to 12 months for someone playing a few times a week. Replace them when the tread under the ball of your foot is worn smooth.
