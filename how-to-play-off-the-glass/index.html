How to play off the glass in padel

Every new player's instinct is to hit the ball before it reaches the glass. Learning to let it come off the wall is the moment padel starts to click.

Quick answer: Turn side-on, move back early to give the ball room, and let it come off the glass towards you. Hit it as it drops in front of you, aiming high and deep to buy time. Don't let it bounce twice.

Why letting the ball pass helps

A deep ball that bounces near your back glass is awkward to hit before it reaches the wall. Let it rebound, and it slows down and comes back towards the middle of the court, where you have more time and better balance.

Playing off the back glass

  1. Read the bounce. If the ball will bounce deep and high, it's coming off the back glass.
  2. Turn side-on early and move back towards the glass, not away from it.
  3. Leave space. Stand far enough from the wall that the ball can rebound in front of you. Beginners usually stand too close.
  4. Let it drop. Hit as the ball falls, at roughly waist height.
  5. Play high and deep. A lob or slow, deep ball gives you time to recover your position.

Playing off the side glass

Balls off the side glass change direction across the court. Keep your eyes on the ball rather than the wall, and move with it. Expect the ball to come off at roughly the same angle it went in.

The double wall

A ball that hits the back glass and then the side glass (or the other way round) rebounds into the middle of the court. Wait a moment longer than feels natural before you move. Rushing in is the most common mistake.

Common mistakes

MistakeFix
Standing too close to the glassGive the ball a metre or more to rebound
Facing the wallStay side-on so you can swing forwards
Swinging hardPlay slow and deep. Defence first
Letting it bounce twiceCommit early once it comes off the glass
Hitting into your own fenceOnly the glass is safe to use on your side

Drills to practise

  • Catch and throw. A partner throws balls at the back glass. Catch each rebound at waist height before you try hitting it.
  • Bounce and lob. Feed balls off the back glass and lob every one deep to the other end.
  • Double-wall waiting. Feed balls into the corner and practise waiting for the second rebound.

Frequently asked questions

Can you hit the ball off your own wall in padel?

Yes. After the ball bounces on your side you can let it come off your glass, and you can hit it against your own glass to send it over the net. You can't use your fence.

Why do pros let the ball go past them?

The rebound slows the ball and brings it back towards the court, giving them more time and a better position for a controlled shot.
