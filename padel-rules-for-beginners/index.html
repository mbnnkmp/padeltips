Padel rules for beginners: how to play

Padel is easy to pick up. If you know tennis scoring, you're halfway there. Here are the rules you need for your first game, including the 2026 updates.

Quick answer: Padel is played in doubles on an enclosed court. Serve underarm after one bounce. The ball can bounce once on your side, then come off your walls before you return it, but your shot must land in the opponents' court before it touches their walls or fence. Scoring is the same as tennis.

The court and the basics

  • Padel is almost always played as doubles: two against two.
  • The court is 20 metres long and 10 metres wide, split by a net and enclosed by glass walls and metal mesh fencing.
  • The walls are part of the game. That's what makes padel different from tennis.

Serving

  • Serve underarm. Drop the ball, let it bounce once behind the service line, and hit it at or below waist height.
  • Serve diagonally into the opponent's service box.
  • You get two attempts, like tennis.
  • After landing in the box, the serve may hit the glass and stay in play. If it hits the metal fence, it's a fault.
  • A serve that clips the net and lands in the right box is a let and is replayed.

The full detail, including the 2026 foot rule, is in our guide to padel serve rules.

Scoring

Padel uses tennis scoring: 15, 30, 40, game. A set is first to six games, winning by two, with a tiebreak at 6-6. Matches are usually best of three sets.

At 40-40 there are three ways to decide the game. Clubs and leagues choose which one they use, so check before you start.

FormatHow it works
AdvantageAs in tennis: win two points in a row from deuce to win the game.
Golden pointOne deciding point at 40-40. The receiving pair chooses which side to receive on.
Star PointAdded by the FIP for 2026. Advantage is played, but if the game keeps returning to deuce, a single deciding point settles it.

Playing off the walls

SituationResult
Your shot lands in their court, then hits their glass or fenceIn play
Your shot hits their glass or fence before bouncingOut. You lose the point
The ball bounces on your side, then comes off your glassYou can still play it
You hit the ball against your own glass so it goes over the netAllowed
You hit the ball into your own fenceYou lose the point
The ball bounces twice on your sideYou lose the point

Volleys and the return of serve

You can volley the ball before it bounces at any time during a rally, with one exception: the return of serve must bounce first.

Common beginner faults

  • Hitting the serve above waist height.
  • Volleying the serve instead of letting it bounce.
  • Hitting the ball straight into the opponents' back glass.
  • Touching the net with your body or racket during a point.
  • Letting the ball bounce twice while waiting for it to come off the glass.

Padel etiquette

  • Bring a fresh tube of balls.
  • Call your own faults and doubles honestly.
  • Wait for the point to finish before walking behind a court.
  • Shake hands, or tap rackets, at the end.

Frequently asked questions

Can you hit the ball off the wall in padel?

Yes, once it has bounced on your side. You can also hit it against your own glass to send it over the net. You can't hit the opponents' walls before the ball bounces in their court.

Can you play padel with two people?

Yes. Some clubs have narrower singles courts, but most padel is doubles.

Is the scoring the same as tennis?

Yes, with the option of golden point or Star Point at 40-40 instead of advantage.

Can you volley the serve in padel?

No. The receiver must let the serve bounce before returning it.
