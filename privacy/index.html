Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics. If you click Accept on our cookie banner, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are read and how people arrive. It sets cookies and collects information like your approximate location, device and browser, and the pages you visit. Google processes this data under Google's privacy policy. If you click Reject, or make no choice, Google Analytics doesn't load and no analytics cookies are set. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings, also linked in the footer of every page.

We store your choice in your browser's local storage, so we don't ask again on every page.
