About Padel Tips

Padel Tips is an independent UK site with one aim: straight, jargon-free advice that helps you buy the right padel gear and enjoy the game more.

Who we are

Padel Tips is written by UK club players.

How we choose products

  • We start from what matters for each level of player: shape, weight and core for rackets, sole and support for shoes, certification for eyewear, consistency and durability for balls.
  • We compare manufacturer specifications, long-term owner reviews and what coaches and club players recommend.
  • We favour gear that's widely available in the UK.
  • Brands can't pay to be included, and they don't see our guides before publication.

Keeping guides up to date

Padel gear changes every year. We review each guide regularly and show the date it was last updated at the top of the page. If you spot something out of date, please let us know.

Health and safety information

Our articles on eye injuries and padel elbow are general information based on published research, not medical advice. If you're injured, speak to a doctor, optician or physiotherapist.

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate, Padel Tips earns from qualifying purchases. When you buy through a link on this site we may earn a small commission, at no extra cost to you. It never changes what we recommend.
