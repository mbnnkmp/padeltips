1. A racket

Most clubs rent rackets, which is the best way to try a few before buying. When you buy, choose a round, light, soft racket.

2. Shoes

For a first session, any clean trainers will do. If you're playing weekly, padel shoes are the most important upgrade, because running shoes are the gear most likely to cause an ankle injury.

4. Eye protection

Optional, but worth considering if you play often. See do you need eye protection for padel?

Nice-to-haves that can wait

  • Overgrips. Cheap and they make a worn grip feel new.
  • A racket cover or bag. Protects the edges from chips.
  • Breathable sportswear and sports socks. Padel courts get hot indoors.
  • A ball saver. Worth it if you open more tubes than you finish.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to play padel for the first time?

Just comfortable sportswear and trainers. Most clubs rent rackets and many provide balls. Check when you book.

What should I buy first?

Shoes, if you're playing regularly. Then a racket that suits your level.
